EKLAVYA CAREER INITIATIVE

Empowering Dreams Through Determination

Inspired by the legendary archer Eklavya—who overcame barriers through sheer determination, self-learning, and dedication—we are committed to supporting students and job seekers who face limited access to quality education and career guidance.

Eklavya did not have a traditional teacher or formal resources, yet he became a master through persistence and self-belief. At Eklavya Career Initiative, we carry forward that legacy by empowering individuals from underserved backgrounds to build their own paths to success.

The Spirit of Eklavya

"Through determination and self-belief, every barrier becomes a stepping stone to greatness."

Our Mission

Through mentorship, skill development programs, and accessible career resources, we aim to level the playing field for those who need it most.

We believe talent exists everywhere—what's often missing is opportunity. Our mission is to bridge that gap, honoring Eklavya's spirit by helping the determined become the exceptional.

AI Internship & Practitioner Programs

Curated, batch-based programs led by Microsoft Certified Trainer Amit Kumar Gupta, designed separately for students (AI Internship) and working professionals (AI Practitioner). Available in foundational and detailed packages depending on requirements.

👨‍🏫

Layman-Friendly Learning

Core concepts of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning explained simply in plain language with zero coding prerequisites.

📊

Data Science Integration

Practical linkages to data science pipelines, data visualization, and strategic business analytics.

💡

Real-World Business Scenarios

Hands-on case studies from daily services: Netflix recommendations, YouTube algorithms, D-Mart shelf management, crop insurance, and Digit car insurance automation.

🤖

RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) Systems

Mastering RAG pipelines using cutting-edge productivity tools: hands-on learning with NotebookLM, building custom Claude Skills, organizing projects in Claude Artifacts, and developing customized GPTs (Gemini Gems / Custom GPTs).

⚙️

Agentic AI & Custom Workflows

Coaching on LLM architectures, prompt design, and tokenization. Transition from static prompting to autonomous Agentic AI and Agentic Workflows to automate repetitive workflows.
